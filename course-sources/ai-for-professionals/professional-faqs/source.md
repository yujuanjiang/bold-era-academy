# Professional FAQs

Answers to the questions professionals ask most about using AI responsibly at work.

## Learning Cards

### Card 1: Can AI write my emails for me?

AI can draft emails, but you should check tone, facts, names, and intent. The best results still sound like you and match the situation.

**Key idea:** AI drafts — you review, personalize, and send.

### Card 2: How do I avoid generic AI output?

Give AI specific context, examples, audience details, and your preferred tone. Ask it to revise toward a clearer goal instead of accepting the first answer.

**Key idea:** Specific input produces specific output.

### Card 3: Can I use AI with confidential work?

Only use approved tools and follow your organization's data rules. Avoid pasting confidential documents, customer data, or internal strategy into unapproved AI systems.

**Key idea:** Approved tools only — know your organization's rules.

### Card 4: How can managers use AI responsibly?

Managers can use AI for planning, summarizing, communication, and analysis while keeping human judgment in decisions about people, performance, and sensitive issues.

**Key idea:** AI supports analysis — humans own people decisions.

### Card 5: Will AI make my work less personal?

It can if you use raw outputs without editing. Use AI to handle structure and first drafts, then add judgment, context, and your own voice.

**Key idea:** Add your voice after the AI draft.

### Card 6: What should I automate first?

Automate low-risk tasks that are repetitive, time-consuming, and easy to review. Good examples include summaries, formatting, status updates, and draft outlines.

**Key idea:** Repetitive, low-risk, easy to review — automate those first.
