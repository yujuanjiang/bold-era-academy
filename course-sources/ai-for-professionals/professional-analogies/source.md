# Analogies & Myths

Reframe how you think about AI at work — and clear up what it cannot do.

## Learning Cards

### Card 1: How should I treat AI at work?

Treat AI as a capable assistant, not a replacement for responsibility. It can speed up preparation, but you own the final result. It is like a junior colleague who drafts quickly but still needs your review.

**Key idea:** You own the result — AI helps you prepare it.

### Card 2: Why does AI need context?

Without context, AI guesses what matters. With context, it can tailor the answer to your audience, goal, and situation. It is like asking for directions without saying where you are starting from.

**Key idea:** No context means guesswork — give AI what it needs.

### Card 3: How can AI reduce busywork?

AI can handle first drafts and repetitive transformations, giving you more time for judgment and relationships. It is like a dishwasher: it saves labor, but you still decide what goes on the table.

**Key idea:** AI handles the labor — you focus on the judgment.

### Card 4: Does using AI mean I am cheating?

Not when it is allowed, transparent when needed, and reviewed carefully. It is similar to using spellcheck, templates, or research tools responsibly.

**Key idea:** Responsible use of tools is professional, not cheating.

### Card 5: Can AI handle workplace nuance alone?

No, AI can miss politics, emotions, history, and hidden context. Use it for support, but keep human judgment for delicate situations.

**Key idea:** Nuance is yours — AI handles the structure.
