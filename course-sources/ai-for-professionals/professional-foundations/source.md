# Professional Foundations

Learn where AI fits in professional work and how to use it without losing your voice.

## Learning Cards

### Card 1: Where can professionals use AI immediately?

AI can help with emails, summaries, research, meeting notes, reports, brainstorming, spreadsheets, presentations, and planning. Start with tasks you repeat every week.

**Key idea:** Start with your most repeated weekly tasks.

### Card 2: What makes a prompt useful at work?

A useful prompt includes context, goal, audience, tone, format, and constraints. The more clearly you define the job, the better the result usually becomes.

**Key idea:** Context + goal + format = a useful work prompt.

### Card 3: How can AI improve writing?

AI can draft, rewrite, shorten, clarify, change tone, and create outlines. You should still review for accuracy, voice, and workplace sensitivity.

**Key idea:** AI handles structure — you handle accuracy and voice.

### Card 4: How can AI support better meetings?

AI can turn notes into summaries, decisions, risks, and action items. It can also help prepare agendas and follow-up messages.

**Key idea:** AI turns messy notes into clean action.

### Card 5: What is workflow thinking with AI?

Workflow thinking means placing AI inside a real work process, not using it as a random chatbot. This helps turn outputs into finished work.

**Key idea:** Embed AI in the workflow — don't treat it as a chatbot.

### Card 6: What is a personal AI playbook?

A personal AI playbook is a short list of prompts and workflows you reuse. It helps you save time consistently instead of starting from zero.

**Key idea:** Save your best prompts — reuse them every week.
