# Investor FAQs

Honest answers to what AI can and cannot do for investors and analysts.

## Learning Cards

### Card 1: Can AI predict the stock market?

AI can find patterns and generate scenarios, but it cannot reliably predict markets with certainty. Markets are affected by human behavior, news, policy, and unexpected events.

**Key idea:** Patterns yes — predictions no.

### Card 2: Should investors trust AI recommendations?

Investors should treat AI recommendations as inputs, not instructions. Check assumptions, data sources, risk, fees, time horizon, and whether the advice fits your goals.

**Key idea:** Inputs to consider — not instructions to follow.

### Card 3: How can analysts use AI safely?

Analysts can use AI to speed research, summarize filings, and generate questions. They should verify numbers, cite sources, and avoid relying on unsupported claims.

**Key idea:** Verify every number — especially AI-generated ones.

### Card 4: Can AI help detect fraud?

Yes, AI can spot unusual patterns, suspicious transactions, or inconsistent claims. It should trigger investigation rather than automatically prove fraud.

**Key idea:** AI flags — humans investigate.

### Card 5: What should investors ask about AI companies?

Ask what AI does, what data it uses, how results are measured, what risks exist, and whether claims are supported by real products or revenue.

**Key idea:** Ask for evidence — products, data, and measured results.

### Card 6: What is a safe first finance AI task?

Start with summarizing public filings, organizing research notes, or creating questions for deeper analysis. Avoid using AI alone for trades or personal financial advice.

**Key idea:** Summaries and research first — not trades or advice.
