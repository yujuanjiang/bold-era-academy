# Analogies & Myths

See through AI hype in finance and understand its real role in investing.

## Learning Cards

### Card 1: How should AI fit into investing?

AI should help gather and organize information, but final decisions need human goals, risk tolerance, and judgment. Faster analysis is not the same as wisdom. It is like a powerful research assistant, not a crystal ball.

**Key idea:** Faster analysis is not wisdom.

### Card 2: Why can AI miss financial risk?

AI may focus on visible patterns while missing context, incentives, or unusual events. Risk often hides outside the data. It is like checking the weather app before sailing: useful, but you still watch the sky.

**Key idea:** Risk hides where the data does not look.

### Card 3: How can AI support portfolio monitoring?

AI can scan changes, flag risks, and summarize updates across holdings. It helps you notice issues faster. It is like a dashboard light in a car: it alerts you, but you still decide what to do.

**Key idea:** AI alerts — you decide.

### Card 4: Does AI remove investment risk?

No, AI can analyze risk but cannot eliminate it. Every investment still involves uncertainty, tradeoffs, and the possibility of loss.

**Key idea:** Risk cannot be automated away.

### Card 5: Are AI-generated numbers always reliable?

No, AI can misread data, use outdated information, or invent unsupported figures. Always verify financial numbers against trusted sources.

**Key idea:** Never trust an AI number without verifying the source.
