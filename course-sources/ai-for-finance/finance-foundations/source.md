## Learning Cards

### Card 1: How is AI used in finance today?

AI helps with research, document review, fraud detection, forecasting, customer support, risk monitoring, and portfolio analysis. It supports professionals but does not remove uncertainty.

**Key idea:** AI supports — it does not remove financial uncertainty.

### Card 2: What can AI do for investment research?

AI can summarize reports, compare companies, extract key risks, scan news, and organize financial information. Human review is essential before making decisions.

**Key idea:** AI speeds research — humans make the decisions.

### Card 3: Why is data quality critical in finance AI?

Financial AI depends on accurate, timely, and relevant data. Bad data can lead to misleading analysis, poor signals, and overconfident decisions.

**Key idea:** Bad data in — bad decisions out.

### Card 4: What is AI risk assessment?

AI risk assessment uses models to identify patterns that may signal financial, credit, market, fraud, or operational risk. It should complement expert judgment.

**Key idea:** AI spots patterns — experts interpret them.

### Card 5: What is AI washing?

AI washing happens when a company exaggerates or misrepresents its use of AI. Investors should look for evidence, not just impressive claims.

**Key idea:** Demand evidence — not just impressive claims.

### Card 6: What is responsible AI use in finance?

Responsible use means checking outputs, protecting private data, understanding model limits, documenting decisions, and keeping humans accountable for high-stakes choices.

**Key idea:** Accountability stays with humans — always.
