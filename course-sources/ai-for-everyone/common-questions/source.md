# Common Questions

Get honest answers to the questions most people have when starting with AI.

## Learning Cards

### Card 1: Can AI replace my own judgment?

AI can support your thinking, but it should not replace your judgment. Use it to explore ideas, compare options, and draft work, then decide what makes sense.

**Key idea:** AI supports judgment — it does not replace it.

### Card 2: Is it safe to share personal information with AI?

Avoid sharing passwords, private financial details, medical records, or sensitive personal information unless you fully trust the tool and understand its privacy rules.

**Key idea:** Protect sensitive information — read the privacy rules first.

### Card 3: Why does AI give different answers sometimes?

AI can respond differently because it generates answers instead of retrieving one fixed response. Small changes in wording can lead to different wording, emphasis, or suggestions.

**Key idea:** AI generates — small changes in input change the output.

### Card 4: Can AI help me learn faster?

Yes, AI can explain topics, quiz you, simplify text, and create examples. It works best when you ask follow-up questions and check important facts.

**Key idea:** AI accelerates learning when you stay curious and verify.

### Card 5: Do I need technical skills to use AI?

No, many AI tools are built for everyday language. You can start by asking for summaries, explanations, drafts, plans, or comparisons.

**Key idea:** Plain language is enough to get started.

### Card 6: What should I try first with AI?

Start with a low-risk task like summarizing an article, rewriting an email, planning a trip, or explaining a confusing term. Practice matters more than perfect prompts.

**Key idea:** Start small — practice builds skill faster than theory.
