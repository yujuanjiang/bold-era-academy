# Core Concepts

Understand what AI is, how it works, and where you already use it every day.

## Learning Cards

### Card 1: Does AI actually understand what I say?

AI does not understand like a person. It finds patterns in language, images, or data and predicts a useful response based on what it has learned.

**Key idea:** AI predicts — it does not understand.

### Card 2: Why does AI sometimes sound so confident?

AI is designed to produce fluent answers, not to feel certainty. It may sound confident even when it is wrong, so important answers should be checked.

**Key idea:** Fluency is not accuracy — always verify important answers.

### Card 3: What makes today's AI different from older software?

Older software follows exact rules written by humans. Modern AI learns patterns from examples, so it can handle flexible tasks like writing, summarizing, and recognizing images.

**Key idea:** Modern AI learns from examples, not just rules.

### Card 4: Where do I already use AI every day?

You likely use AI in maps, search engines, shopping recommendations, spam filters, voice assistants, translation tools, and photo apps. Much of it works quietly in the background.

**Key idea:** AI is already woven into your daily tools.

### Card 5: What is a prompt?

A prompt is the instruction or question you give to an AI tool. Clear prompts usually produce clearer answers because they give the AI better direction.

**Key idea:** Clear prompts lead to clearer answers.

### Card 6: What is a good first AI habit?

Ask AI to explain its reasoning, list assumptions, or show alternatives. This turns AI from an answer machine into a thinking partner.

**Key idea:** Ask for reasoning, not just answers.
