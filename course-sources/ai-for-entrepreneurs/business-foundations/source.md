# Business Foundations

Understand where AI creates real value and how to choose the right place to start.

## Learning Cards

### Card 1: Where can AI save a business time first?

AI often helps fastest with repetitive writing, customer replies, meeting summaries, research, data cleanup, and simple planning. Start where work is frequent and time-consuming.

**Key idea:** Start with frequent, time-consuming, and repeatable tasks.

### Card 2: What is an AI use case?

An AI use case is a specific business task where AI can create value. Good use cases have a clear problem, user, workflow, and measurable benefit.

**Key idea:** A good use case is specific and measurable.

### Card 3: How should a business choose an AI tool?

Choose based on the job you need done, not the hype. Look at accuracy, privacy, ease of use, integrations, cost, and how well it fits your workflow.

**Key idea:** Match the tool to the job — ignore the hype.

### Card 4: What does AI automation mean?

AI automation means using AI to complete or assist repeatable tasks with less manual effort. It works best when the task has clear inputs, steps, and review points.

**Key idea:** Automation works best with clear inputs and review points.

### Card 5: Why should businesses start small with AI?

Small pilots reduce risk and reveal what actually works. A focused experiment is easier to measure, improve, and scale than a broad AI transformation project.

**Key idea:** Small pilots teach more than big plans.

### Card 6: What is an AI policy?

An AI policy is a simple guide for how people in your business may use AI. It should explain approved tools, data rules, review steps, and accountability.

**Key idea:** Simple rules protect your business and build trust.
